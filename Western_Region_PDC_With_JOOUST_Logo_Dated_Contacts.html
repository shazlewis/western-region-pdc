FAQ

Who can attend?

Students, staff and innovators from institutions in the Western region, plus invited industry guests.

Is there a registration fee?

See the organisers’ announcement for the latest details on fees and what they cover.

Do I need a project to attend?

No. You can register as an observer. Presenters should complete the project section.

Can I register as a team?

Yes. One member registers the project and lists the other members. Each team member should also register individually.

Can I edit my submission later?

Contact the organisers with your reference number and they will update it.
